Read these numbers as a floor

  • Counts are confirmed uses, not every use. fac- matched 798 headwords by spelling; 103 had an etymology that names facere. The real family is larger.
  • Greek stems are undercounted. The Gutenberg transcription dropped Greek script, so stems like tele-, bio- and geo- could not be checked and are excluded rather than ranked last.
  • This measures word-building, not how often people say the words. Usage frequency in speech or text would need a corpus such as Google Books Ngrams.
  • Dictionary age matters. Webster's 1913 predates most of the techn-, gnos- and migr- vocabulary in use today, which is part of why those sit at the bottom.

Source: Webster's Revised Unabridged Dictionary (1913), public domain, Project Gutenberg eBook #29765. Stem table and matching rules are shared with Etymon Tracer, where any stem below can be searched in Root mode.
